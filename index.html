💪 Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body is like a super car — the more you use it,the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes every day are happier,sleep better,and do better at school.

Dancing,Swimming,Football,Yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster counts.Keep moving and have fun!

☀ Quick Health Tips

    🍎 Eat 5 fruits and veggies every day 💧 Drink 8 glasses of water every day 😴 Sleep 9 to 11 hours every night 🏃 Move your body for 60 minutes
